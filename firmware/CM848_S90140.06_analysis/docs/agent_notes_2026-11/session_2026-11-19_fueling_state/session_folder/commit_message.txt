cm848: FUELING_STATE enum - governor_fuel_demand_mode is the governed fueling state

governor_fuel_demand_mode (0x40a154) is the Cummins FSGVFLST governed fueling
state. Its 35 accesses are all 16-bit whole-value compares or copies, and only
the arbitration in cm848_processProtectionRampControl writes it. Its codes
come from one numbering that every fueling stage shares (e2m FSDSFLST 1-8 /
FSGVFLST 1-12 / FSLMFLST 13-41), so the enum is shared:

- ENGINE_OPERATING_MODE -> FUELING_STATE (members renamed), plus 4 power
  take-off (e2m-derived) / 9 cruise control / 10 vehicle speed limit /
  11 low speed governor / 12 high speed governor
- typed FUELING_STATE: engine_operating_mode,
  engine_operating_mode_previous_pass, operating_mode_governor_output_t
  .mode_code, 0x40b67c, 0x3fb526, governor_fuel_demand_mode,
  governor_fuel_demand_mode_prev, cruise_fuel_demand_mode_prev
- 0x40b67c governor_pid_integral_snapshot -> governor_fuel_demand_working_mode
- the 0x3fb526 dword split into governor_fuel_demand_working_mode_prev and
  fuel_demand_enable_sync_flag_shadow (word)
