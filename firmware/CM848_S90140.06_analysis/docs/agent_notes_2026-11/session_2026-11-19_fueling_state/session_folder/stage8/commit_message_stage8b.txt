cm848: the HPCR gain records as structs - piecewise_linear_gain_t / derivative_gain_t (stage 8b)

cm848_applyPiecewiseLinearGain and cm848_integrateWithZoneGain read a 16-byte record (three
dword gains - small signal / positive large / negative large - then the signed positive and
negative window widths); cm848_calculateFilteredDerivative reads an 18-byte one (a word filter
coefficient first). The ECU's parameter ID table resolves seven e2m blocks of exactly those
sizes to the records, and the dump reads in range at the field widths:
- fuel_pressure_governor_proportional_gains / _integral_gains / _derivative_gains (8014 /
  8015 / 80EF);
- fuel_metering_current_compensation_proportional_gains / _integral_gains /
  _derivative_gains (8016 / 8017 / 80F2; the P gains and the derivative record are all 0 on
  this truck) and fuel_metering_uncompensated_duty_cycle_proportional_gains (8018).

Before, the records were 23 flat rows, several wrong: the 80EF dword gains were word rows over
their high halves plus _lo_cal rows over the low halves, 8016-8018 / 80F2 were byte rows named
cam_sync_*_prot_*_cal, and the window pairs were dwords with +2 label rows. The three consumers
have full prototypes now (sword return, int32_t input, q16_fixed_point_t * state). The stack
copies in cm848_calculateFuelMeteringCommand are typed (working_*_gains), and its plate explains
the one store that runs past the derivative record.

Proof: exactly the four predicted functions changed (the three consumers and
cm848_calculateFuelMeteringCommand), plus one local declaration reorder elsewhere; the warnings
and type stats are identical to stage 4; verify is deterministic; headers compile; the script
tests pass. Also: wave 13's 92 quoted dump values re-read (all right), and the 80B9 short to
ground / short to battery caveat on its six rows.
