cm848: round 63 stage C - the boot kernel's PGN 61184 services: BOOT_DIAGNOSTIC_SERVICE, fixed read prototypes

The boot kernel's twin of stages A / B. Its handlers dispatch through cm848_dispatchDiagnosticService, which already
takes a j1939_rx_msg_t *request.

- BOOT_DIAGNOSTIC_SERVICE (16 members). It is its own enum because the boot kernel's 7 downloads a calibration,
  where the application's 7 exits development mode. It holds the 14 services the boot kernel registers and the
  0x0c / 0x0d responses (cm848_sendDiagServiceResponse sends the same bytes as the application's sender).
  Typed on cm848_registerDiagnosticService's service_id (all 14 registrations render member names, values and
  handlers unchanged in order), on boot_diagnostic_service_entry_t.service_id and on the dispatcher's service local.
- Wrong prototypes fixed:
  - The read handler (0x1b48) was (address uint, length ushort, response_ptr byte*). The body reads its first
    parameter as the request (+6 / +4 / +0 / +2 / +3) and compares the third with the 1764 cap. It is (request,
    address, length) now. Its CONCAT22(in_register_00000010, length) is gone, and the 0x4a handler passes the full
    address instead of its low half (wStack_e).
  - The read-by-ID worker (0x1cf8) named its third parameter reserved and its fourth offset. Its add r31,r4,r31
    adds the third to the address, and the fourth goes on as the length. It is (request, parameter_id, offset,
    length) now, and the (short)iVar1 truncation is gone.
  - The write twins and the 8 memory and address handlers take the request too.
- 23 functions renamed on the application's scheme with "Boot". CM550 has none of the old names.
  - The 5 registrars: their old names had "init", "Diag" and "Standard".

    cm848_diagService67_memoryReadOffset: the 4 bytes are the length. Service 0x19 is
