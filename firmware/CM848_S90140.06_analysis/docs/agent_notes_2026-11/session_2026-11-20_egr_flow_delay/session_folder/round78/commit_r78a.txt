cm550: round 78 stage A - the last 16 structs with a separate address per field row removed; their globals and the neighbors they covered typed

Round 77 removed the J1708 pair. The other 16 CM550 structs whose field rows each carry their own address are removed
now (68 rows); multi_address_structs.py reports none in either firmware.
- Each became one oversize type with an instance at every listed address. The globals that carried the type rendered
  fake fields:
  - previous_engine_rpm.previous_sample was dma_desc1_source_ptr (0x800010);
  - engine_rpm_delta.rpm_delta was data_collector_channel_count (0x80034c);
  - logical_address_canter_value_..._none.ram_end_addr was logical_address_of_the_last_value_stored_to_the_data_
    collector_bu_none (0x80aaba);
  - fuel_arbitrator_throttle_override.* were its neighbors (0x80d038 ...).
  Only four of the 16 rendered; the other twelve could flip later, as param_circular_buffer_t did in round 76.
- Every field row's address but three had its own global. The global gets the field's scalar width; the globals that
  carried a removed type get it too. The three addresses without a global get type-only rows.
- The diagnostic transmit / receive buffers (0x808B00 / 0x808C00) are byte[256]; nothing else lies in their range. The
  32-byte "data_buffer" at 0x80965E is not typed: globals start 2 bytes later.

Two follow-ups from reading the first build:
- The neighbors the oversize types had covered became visible with underscore prefixes (types too narrow). They are
  typed from their decoded m68k accesses:
  - dma_descriptor_base1, dma_desc1_source_ptr, data_collector_sample_decimation_counter and
    data_collector_channel_count are words;
  - logical_address_of_the_last_value_..._none is a dword.
- Five coordinate rows named addresses as fields of the removed types (previous_engine_rpm.rpm_delta,
  engine_rpm_delta.previous_nonzero, ...logical_start / +2, diagnostic_rx_buffer_base.rx_buffer_base[220]). Four
  become type-only rows of the measured width. The fifth (0x808DDC) goes, because its accesses do not decode.
- io_control_bit0_state (0x80D03E) is a dword. The removed fuel_arbitrator_diag_t rendered all 15 of its accesses
  as fuel_arbitrator_throttle_override._8_4_, 4 bytes at 0x80D036 + 8. Three functions add the 32-bit
  _io_control_handshake_state into it with an overflow check. 0x80D040 (io_control_bit1_state, label-only) lies
  inside it and has no access of its own. The io_control_* names are older and not checked.

Proof:
- CM550 verify is DETERMINISTIC after each of the three steps. The scan reports no multi-address struct in either
  firmware.
- prove_r78a_cm550.py maps each of the old 'global.field' renders (11 forms) to the address it reached, prints the name
  that address has now, and compares the functions statement by statement. 11 functions differ, and every change is
  one of these:
  - a mapped field becomes the real global's name;
  - an old raw or piece render (ram0x00800012, DAT_00800348, DAT_0080aabe._0_2_, ._10_4_) becomes its type-only
    row's label;
  - an underscore is dropped.
- Over the round, no underscore-prefixed name is gained and three are lost (the data collector's buffer addresses).
- The headers regenerate with 0 g++ errors. The _cal audit reports HARD 0 and MISSING 31. test_lookup_tables and
  test_rename_waves pass.
