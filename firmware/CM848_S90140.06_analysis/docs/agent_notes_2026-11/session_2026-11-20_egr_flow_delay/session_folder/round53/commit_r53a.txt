cm848: round 53 stage A - eleven more e2m blocks record for record; the sensor-fault substitutes named by their fault

semantic_record_matches.py is round 52's matcher, ranked by a token-overlap meaning score (semantic_score.py: e2m
abbreviations expanded, the record name and description against the row name). Each block's best base was then read
by meaning:
- 8B20 (RAM 0x40b2f4): the fan speed sensor words, the fan's twin of the transmission's 8BB4.
- 160A / 1609 (RAM 0x40b9c8 / ROM 0x64302): the PTO-disengaged and battery-voltage-in-range flags and their
  calibrations.
- 8B03 / 8B04 (0x40868a / RAM 0x40b14e): the sensor-fault substitutes and the in-use values.
  - The eight substitutes are now named by the fault that selects them, as the e2m's High_Default / Low_Default are.
  - cm848_selectSensorValuesInUse uses coolant_temperature_low_substitute on the HIGH fault, so the coolant and intake
    temperature pair went by value while the pressure and pedal pair went by fault. All eight are now
    <sensor>_high_fault_substitute / _low_fault_substitute.
- 8BB1 (0x408706): the learned output-speed-to-vehicle-speed ratio limits.
- 8B8A (0x408b78): the road speed governor's rail-leak limit, and a new row drivetrain_gear_ratio_detection_margin
  (Fractional_GR_Difference, the margin cm848_updateDrivetrainRatio adds to the top-gear and gear-down ratios).
- 8A7D (RAM 0x40b4ff): the J1850 PCI received data.
  - pci_receive_message_13_state_2_flag -> pci_four_wheel_drive_status_flag (J1850_4WD_Status).
  - The middle of the block holds the immobilizer words this build adds.
- 8A86 (RAM 0x3fcd88): the brake switch pair counters.
- 8ADA (RAM 0x40b5a8): the vehicle speed words. That is the semantic anchor round 51's 0x40b5a8 lead needed.
- 8ADB (EEPROM mirror 0x3fd8f0): the vehicle speed sensor calibrations.
- 8B0B (0x408d1c): the transfer case switch learning.
- Records with no access get no row: 8A7D +05 / +1A and 8ADB +0E, each listed on its block's first row.
- None of the renamed names exist in CM550.
- The matcher's top hit 8B7C (AFR) exists only in older layouts and its meanings disagree with the rows, so it was
  not applied.
