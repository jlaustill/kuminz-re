cm848: round 41 stage A - the injector driver booster capacitor monitor, its ROM records typed as structs

cm848_processFaultScanLookup (0x35fa8) is the injector driver booster capacitor monitor, now
cm848_monitorInjectorDriverBoosterCapacitorVoltage. engine_injectionAngle_tpuChannel6EventHandler runs it for each
cylinder event, and it reads that cylinder's capacitor channel (54 / 55) through a ROM record. Its tables were
loose rows.
- **injector_driver_booster_capacitor_monitor_record_t[2] at 0x5762c** (52 bytes each, decoded from the ROM), one
  record per capacitor. Each record holds:
  - the sensor channel;
  - pointers to the channel config's high / low threshold and debounce limit;
  - the high / low fault numbers, 328 / 329 and 330 / 331 (booster_capacitor_N_voltage_high / low_error);
  - pointers to the channel state's raw / value / held value;
  - the linearization axis / values pointers;
  - the high / low / spare count pointers;
  - the low voltage mask (1 / 2).

  These replace 12 loose rows. Two of them, j1939_pgn_lookup_ptr / j1939_message_def_ptr bytes, were the fault
  numbers.
- **injector_driver_booster_capacitor_cylinder_record_t[8] at 0x57694** (10 bytes each; was the word
  calibration_constant_1). Each record holds the capacitor index and the four injector circuit faults that skip the
  cylinder: fast decay, open circuit, short to ground, short to battery.
  - Records 0-5 are injectors 1-6, and their capacitors alternate 0 / 1.
  - The table ends at the watchdog answer table.
- **The RAM:**
  - the shared linearization lookup cache (0x3fb228);
  - per-capacitor word[2] counts: high (0x3fb22a), low (0x3fb22e), spare (0x3fb232; cleared only);
  - per-cylinder word[8] counts. Three rows were renamed and widened: per_cylinder_timing_array (a byte),
    per_cylinder_sync_counter and per_cylinder_timing_counter. They are now the high / low fault debounce counts and
    the low voltage counts.
  - injection_check_flags -> injector_driver_booster_capacitor_low_voltage_mask. It holds the low capacitor's mask,
    which the cutout logic compares with 1 / 2 / 0.
- **Functions:** mpc555_initSensorCalibrationTables -> cm848_initializeInjectorDriverBoosterCapacitorMonitor, and the
  monitor's plate describes its three checks: review-function-readability, Readability Medium (temps and one goto
  remain), Accuracy High.

Proof:
- prove_records.py maps the old raw addresses and the new struct accesses to field tokens, by the field at each
  offset. The monitor then has 112 statements on each side, identical apart from the function name.
- The initializer was checked address by address.
- The other 1966 functions match the label-only canonical form or differ only in the order of local declarations.
- lookup_tables.py: 920 typed, 0 sizing problems. Thunks 18 / 14. Headers regenerated with the two types (0 g++
  errors), tests pass. CM550 has neither function name.
