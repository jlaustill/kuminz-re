An advisor review of the round 85 closing found these.
- Task 046 and the PR body said stage A's 23 names split 11 mc68k_ / 12 cm550_. They split 14 mc68k_ / 9 cm550_; 12 is
  the number of proposals kept off mc68k_ (8 renamed with cm550_, 4 left as they were).
- Before this commit, a byte scan checked the watchdog-only split against the ROM, since the .cpp drops blocks:
  rom_hardware_operands.py looks for 0x00FFxxxx operands other than the two watchdog registers. The 12 functions are
  clean. The two hits are an andi.l immediate (0x3664) and flash parameter block data past the boot block's last rts.
  mc68k_flashEraseBlock's 0xFFD000 and mc68k_delayTimeBaseTicksWithWatchdogService's 0xFFF404 (CTM4_BIUTBR) show as
  expected.
- Task 046 leads:
  - the survey's class Z: 0xFBE4, a fragment of 0xFB26; 0x1B4C2, an interrupt epilogue fragment; and 10 call targets
    with no function;
  - the round 86 stack argument fix. The three research reports are archived in session_folder/round85/.
- Comments that still named the renamed J1708 dispatcher:
  - four '#' section headers in CM550's enums.csv (ApplyEnums.java skips '#' lines, so the build output cannot change);
  - a note in work/docs/clip-protocol-spec.md: 0x12484 is the J90280.05 address of
    mc68k_dispatchJ1708ReceivedMessages (0x15F8E).

  has a relocation_map.csv. Only CM550 has one, and it is the historical J90280.05 -> J90350.00 map (that firmware's

  CM550 -> CM848. The section now says what the map is, and that firmware/cross_firmware_matches.csv lists scored
  CM550 -> CM848 candidates (all 189 rows are 'review').
