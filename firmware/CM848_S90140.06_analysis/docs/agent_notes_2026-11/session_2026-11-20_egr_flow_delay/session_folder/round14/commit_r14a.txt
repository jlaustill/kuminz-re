cm848: round 14 stage A - the low speed governor reference speed chain from e2m 14EE / 1496 / 14EF / 14F0 (label-only)

The LSG reference speed chain (round 13's first lead) carried governor_speed_limit_* / thermal_governor_* /
protection names. The Cummins e2m blocks lie past the end of the dumped parameter ID table, so they are aligned by
relative offset, and every record checks against its code use and the dump values.
- **14EE (ROM settings) at 0x4070ee:** all 32 records +0..+3E fit, and 8C76 follows at 0x40712e (11.46 drops +40 /
  +42).
  - The cold / hot idle coolant temperatures and timers, the A/C / drive / park idle speeds.
  - The extended idle conditions (coolant, intake manifold and battery temperatures, road speed) and its speed.
  - The idle increment / decrement rates, the minimum / maximum / maximum tool idle speeds, the J1850 and J1939
    enables, and the altitude slew rate (4 bytes, as the e2m says) and hystereses.
  - The three *_PN_Enable flags read backwards (0 = park / neutral at rest only), so they are named
    *_outside_park_neutral_enable.
  - Switch 13 is e2m 1612 AC_On_Off_Switch.
- **1496 (RAM) at 0x40aeb2:** all 16 records fit.
  - The reference before droop, the desired reference speed, and the coolant / park-or-drive / extended idle / A/C /
    J1850 / tool / moving speed requests, plus the extended idle status.
  - The struct at +10 (was governor_output_t / governor_output_t_0040aec2) is low_speed_governor_reference_t: reference_speed,
    no_load_fuel, reference_speed_state (e2m +14 LSG_Reference_Speed_State) and the 4-byte altitude_speed.
  - +1A SCI_Cmd19_Status is round 13's 0xE5C1 key.
- **14EF (ROM) at 0x5c4ae** (it ends where 8A51 starts) and **14F0 (RAM) at 0x40aede:** the LSG error filter
  coefficient / gain / clamps, the adjusted reference speed, and the derivative and jerk terms.
- **The functions:**
  - The task is cm848_updateLowSpeedGovernorReferenceSpeed (was cm848_updateGovernorSpeedLimits). It runs
    cm848_calculateLowSpeedGovernorCoolantTemperatureSpeed, then cm848_selectLowSpeedGovernorDesiredReferenceSpeed
    (was cm848_calculateProtectionSpeedLimits), then cm848_slewLowSpeedGovernorReferenceSpeed (was
    cm848_applySpeedLimitConstraints; the thermal_governor_* words were its rates).
  - Also the error terms, the initializer and the no-load fuel.
- **The tables:** coolant (8A51 / 8A52), altitude (8B16 / 8B17 by role) and moving (8C51 / 8C52). New rows: the
  14EF gain and clamps.
- **Left for stage C:** the 0x3faf7a dword is two words (sth / lhz only). Left out: the droop offset at 0x40aedc
  (only ever cleared).
