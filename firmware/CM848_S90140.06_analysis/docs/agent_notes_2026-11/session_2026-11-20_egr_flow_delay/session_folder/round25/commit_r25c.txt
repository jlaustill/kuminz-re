cm848: round 25 stage C - the executive frame timing records and their pointers (types)

A round 24 lead. With algorithm_enable_flags_18_cal.executive_timing_enable set, main_loop times every pass:
- When the enable rises, it clears 12-byte records from 0x40be6a up to ram_refresh_enable. That is 40 records, and
  nothing else lives in the range.
- After each pass it adds the pass's timebase ticks to the record its walking pointer points at (sum, count,
  minimum, maximum, overflows above the threshold), and an overflow also counts e2m 12A6 Executive_Timing_Overflows
  in EEPROM.
- It steps the pointer by one record, and phase 0 of its 11-phase switch resets it.
- scheduler_task_stat_t -> executive_frame_timing_t (type-only): call_count / min_time / max_time / overrun_count ->
  frame_count / minimum_ticks / maximum_ticks / overflow_count. The instance becomes the array executive_frame_timings
  (executive_frame_timing_t[40]), and its 4 dotted alias rows go.
- The two walking pointers are executive_frame_timing_t * now (executive_frame_timing_pointer /
  _clear_pointer), so *(ushort *)(p + 8) etc. read p->maximum_ticks / ->minimum_ticks / ->total_ticks /
  ->frame_count / ->overflow_count, and the pointer steps by + 1.
- The helpers: executive_frame_ticks / executive_frame_start_ticks / executive_timing_enabled_previous_flag /
  executive_frame_overflow_threshold_ticks / executive_timing_overflow_count_cal. The old offset alias row
  timer_base_snapshot+2 goes, and 0x3feec0 gets its e2m row (12A6 +2 Executive_Frame_Max_Time_Capture, no reader).
