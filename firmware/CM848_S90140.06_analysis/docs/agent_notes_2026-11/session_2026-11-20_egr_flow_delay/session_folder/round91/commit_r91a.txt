Workflow wf_368d2db4-707 ran an investigator and a skeptic per item (10 agents). 216 elements were confirmed and 11
amended (comment text only); none were refuted.

- S1, the fault count table (e2m 1031 DGFUCUBF): fault_count_record_t (10 bytes: status_change_time /
  last_countdown_time / status / occurrence_count, from the 44 accesses in 13 functions) and fault_count_table[125]
  at 0x805a1c. It replaces the 3 field rows and 27 unreferenced J90280.05-map rows inside it (io_fault_status_reg_1,
  vp44_fso_fault_flags, rpm_histogram_buffer_start, ...).
  - cm550_phase2_sensor_data_processor (0x1452e) is a fall-through split of the countdown function and goes to
    deleted_functions.csv.
  - The fault countdown functions are named in both firmwares as counterparts: cm550_countDownPreviouslyActiveFault(s),
    cm550_processFaultScanGroup and cm550_resetFaultStatus; CM848 cm848_countDownPreviouslyActiveFault(s) (were
    cm848_faultTimeoutHandler / cm848_activeFaultScanProcessor).
- S2, the user datalogger (e2m 1282 / 1283), with the record arrays in the EEPROM mirror:
  - datalogger_trigger_t[4] (16 bytes) at 0x803d64 and datalogger_data_parameter_t[5] (6 bytes) at 0x803da4;
  - datalogger_trigger_information_t, its working RAM (the sample ring buffer, the resolved addresses, the latches and
    flags);
  - 9 functions (cm550_updateDataloggerTriggers, cm550_recordDataloggerSamples, ...).
  The legacy names those words had (scheduler_phase_execution_time, fuel_demand_blend_input, flash_write_data_pointer,
  ...) and 63 rows inside the new arrays are gone. 0x800358 main_loop_phase_index was one of them: the scheduler's
  phase index is scheduler_phase_index (0x80068c), so docs/cross_firmware_variable_map.csv,
  docs/cross-firmware-analysis.md and an enums.csv comment are corrected.
- S3, the all-speed governor gain sets: CM550 takes CM848's all_speed_governor_gain_set_t (the same 9 signed words)
  for j1939_tsc1_speed_control_condition_gain_sets[4], all_speed_governor_throttle_mode_gain_set and
  all_speed_governor_application_gain_set, and all_speed_governor_gain_set_pointer (was asg_governor_gains_ptr).

    receivePgn61443ElectronicEngineController2Message / receivePgn65252ShutdownMessage /
