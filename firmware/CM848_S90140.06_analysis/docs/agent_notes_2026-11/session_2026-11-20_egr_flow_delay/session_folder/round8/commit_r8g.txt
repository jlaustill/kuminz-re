cm848: round 8 stage F - e2m 8C18 (48RE ROM cals) and the exhaust brake lockup aid; stage E corrected (label-only)

A review asked for the e2m description at 8C17 +4A before accepting stage E's "converter clutch engagement
monitor". The description is 'Used to time ebrake TCC inhibit timeout limit', and it led to e2m 8C18. That block
aligns word by word at 0x4082f4.
- **8C18 (ROM cals) at 0x4082f4, +00..+98.** From +9A the rows are a table axis. Rows named before from their code
  use confirm it: +0E RE_ERPM_WOT_Target (stage E's WOT upshift target, 24600), +16 RE_Lockup_Boost (the pressure
  state 0x10 target), +1E RE_Low_Range_Max, +20..+24 RE_Max_2 / 3 / 4_Ratio, +26..+54 the overdrive and
  over-temperature light temperatures, +62 RE_Road_Speed_Filter_Constant, and +70 RE_OSS_Accel_Method (the method
  switch of cm848_calculateOutputShaftAcceleration). 71 globals are named, each checked against its row's users, among
  them:
  - the engine acceleration default / maximum / minimum, normal and low range (were protection_load_scaled_*_cal);
  - the WOT upshift target low / high band and the shift lag step (were protection_rpm_peak_* /
    governor_pressure_learned_gain_step);
  - the predicted 1-2 / 2-3 shift delay limits (were governor_pressure_learned_gain_gear1 / 2_maximum / _minimum);
  - the lockup, overheat lockup and relock delays (were protection_*_lockout_* / *_countdown_* cals), and the new row
    transmission_overheat_lockup_delay_4_3_time at 0x408370;
  - the throttle detent and WOT thresholds (were protection_fuel_demand_status10 / 80_*);
  - the overheat protection temperatures (were hot_mode_*) and the governor pressure override (state 0x17);
  - governor_pressure_lockup_boost / _2nd_gear_lockup_boost (were governor_pressure_high_gear_target /
    _gear2_locked_target).
- **The exhaust brake lockup aid (corrects stage E).**
  - The records: 8C17 +4A RE_E_Brake_Inhibit_Timer, 8C18 +88..+92 RE_EB_Lockup_Range_* ('If ERPM/OSS > this ratio,
    Set RE_E_Brake_Inhibit_LU = FALSE'), 8C18 +96 RE_E_Brake_Inhibit_Timeout and 8C19 +16 RE_E_Brake_Inhibit_LU ('Flag
    set to inhibit Exhaust Brake to allow TCC lockup').
  - The code: cm848_updateEngineRetarderEnableState reads bit 0 of 0x40b0fe as its torque converter lockup disengaged
    input, and the exhaust brake sets that bit entering 2nd gear and after a 4-3 downshift.
  - The renames: cm848_updateExhaustBrakeLockupInhibit (was cm848_monitorConverterClutchEngagement),
    exhaust_brake_lockup_flags (was converter_clutch_transition_state), exhaust_brake_lockup_inhibit_timer /
    _timeout / _previous and exhaust_brake_lockup_speed_ratio_2nd / 3rd / 4th_gear_minimum / _maximum.
  - 8C17 drifts from +4C, not +4A. e2m 8C19 (the RE flag bytes) aligns at 0x40b0e8, the
    protection_condition_flags_t struct. Its bits are the next lead.
- **The lockup boost:** cm848_selectGovernorPressureLockupBoost (was stage E's cm848_selectGovernorPressureHighGear).
  Pressure state 0x10 is RE_Lockup_Boost, 'Gov pressure target during lockup, to prevent downshift'.
- **Switch 12 is the exhaust brake switch.** exhaust_brake_transmission_features_inhibit (RE_Ebrake_Inhibit, was
  protection_fuel_check_bypass_flag) gates its alternate shift lines.
