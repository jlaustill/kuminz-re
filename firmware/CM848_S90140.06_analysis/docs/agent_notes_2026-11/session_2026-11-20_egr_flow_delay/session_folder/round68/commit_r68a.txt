cm848: round 68 stage A - the application's EEPROM mirror words at 0x3fee18 typed from e2m (fuel level, intake air heater, engine-off time)

The boot kernel's eeprom_header_t ("eeprom_shadow_header", 0x3fee08) ran to 0x3fee29 over words that the application
reads as its own EEPROM mirror (RAM 0x3fd482 + EEPROM offset). So 13 application functions rendered the fuel level,
intake air heater and engine-off time words as eeprom_shadow_header.calibration_block1[N] / eeprom_status_flags /
cal_word2 / cal_word3. e2m names the words (EEPROM 0x1996-0x19a1), and the asm gives their widths: lbz at
0x3fee18 / 19 / 1a, and lhz / sth at 0x3fee1c / 1e / 20 / 22.
- eeprom_header_t ends at 0x3fee17 (config_word).
- fuel_level_saved_values_t at 0x3fee18 = e2m 8A24. Its fields are in_range_filtered_percent, last_valid_percent,
  percent_tank_full_average (sent by PCI TX[13] header 0xA4) and dummy_byte_1. e2m calls that last byte its own
  "space filler to prevent compiler complaints", so the source has a struct here.
- intake_air_heater_saved_values_t at 0x3fee1c = e2m 8A36: inhibit_postheat_start_count and
  redundant_preheat_request. The end-of-line start step's single sth (0x19, 25 engine starts) had rendered as two byte
  stores.
- engine_off_time_saved_values_t at 0x3fee20 = e2m 8C36 (Chry_Dat42_Previous / _Stuck_Times): previous_trip_value
  and unchanged_trip_count, cm848_latchAndCheckEngineOffTime's check of the PCI engine-off time.
- The boot kernel time-shares these words: its copies of EEPROM 0x4a-0x4f and 0x50-0x54 land there. Three boot
  plates now say where the application's names render: mpc555_verifyDownloadedCalibration, cm848_beginSerialReflash,
  and cm848_loadCalibrationFromEeprom, which gets its first plate.
- 0x3fee24 / 0x3fee26 are boot only and become boot_serial_reflash_unlock_request / boot_eeprom_status_block. The old
  field name "serial_number" was wrong: the word is a copy of EEPROM 0x0c, and the serial number is EEPROM 0x8e.
- cm848_lookupEepromParameterAddress gets a prototype (byte * / parameter) and a plate. Once a struct started at
  0x3fee1c, its one path returning that address made Ghidra type its return as the struct pointer and cast the other
  paths. Its only caller casts the result to byte *.
- firmware/CLAUDE.md line 146: the reference refresh renamed eeprom_shadow_header.serial_reflash_unlock_request to
  boot_serial_reflash_unlock_request (a factual rename).
