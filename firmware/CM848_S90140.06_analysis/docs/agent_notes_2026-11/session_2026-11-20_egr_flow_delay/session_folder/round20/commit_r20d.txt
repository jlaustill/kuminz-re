cm848: round 20 stage D - 17 algorithm enable flags words as bitfields (types)

algorithm_enable_flags_<N>_t word bitfields on words 1, 2, 5, 6, 13, 19, 20, 21, 25, 27, 28, 29, 31, 32, 40, 42 and
43. Every code use of those words was a single-bit masked read; the 200 reads now name the bit, e.g.
algorithm_enable_flags_42_cal.fuel_pressure_tracking_error_monitor_enable. Word 0 stays a word, because the engine
protection loops index the block from it. Each bit's comment carries its e2m 106B record, its readers and its value on
this truck.

Naming tiers, enforced by apply_r20d.py:
- 117 read bits are named. 113 take their record's name, and the reader agrees with it. 4 have no record and are named
  from their behaviour: word 29 0x1000 (the PGN 65263 fuel delivery pressure byte), word 40 0x4000 / 0x8000 (the
  retarder fault mask / PTO inhibit), and word 42 0x1000 (the gear ratio torque management; the e2m's
  Torque_Mgmt_GR_Enable sits on 0x800, which nothing reads).
- 50 unread bits are named from their record, each with exactly one record whose description spells the words out.
- 105 bits are reserved_bit_N, with the record in the comment. This covers the unrecorded bits and the never-spelled
  initialisms SS / CSE / VGA / QPID / OOG / TVA. Word 21 bit 15 and word 29 bits 0 / 1 are read but stay reserved,
  with their readers in the comment.
- Where two records share a bit (word 19 0x200 / 0x2000), the reader picks. Word 32's Equivalent_Throttle_Enable
  (mask 7) goes by its value list, where ENABLE = 4, and the code tests 4.
- The dump agrees with the truck: word 40 j1850_communication_enable (the PCI bus), word 43
  vacuum_throttle_cruise_enable and wastegate_solenoid_driver_enable, word 6 transmission_control_enable (the 48RE).

Also cold_delta_fuel_cached_axis_offsets (0x3fb35c, word[2]), the lookup cache of cold_delta_fuel_grid.

Proof: normalize_r20d.py rewrites each field read back to its mask (the map comes from structure_definitions.csv). Its
count gate passes: per word and per mask, old masked reads = new field reads, and no masked read or piece is left.
cast_normalized_diff.py then shows two changes. mpc555_initFuelDemandOffsetFilter writes the cache as [0] / [1]
(intended). mpc555_tickTransferTimeoutAndRuntimeCounter only reorders a local declaration. The piece count stays
464 -> 464. Headers regenerated (0 g++ errors), lookup_tables.py 0 sizing problems, the four test suites pass.
