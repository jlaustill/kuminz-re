cm848: round 36 stage A - the J1939 EFL/P1 and PGN 65504 payloads as structs (types)

Two more transmit payloads of flat rows became structs in the J1939-71 order.
- j1939_pgn65263_payload_t (EFL/P1, 8 bytes at 0x3fadd9), with SPN names from the automotive table:
  - The builder writes bytes 1 / 4 / 7: fuel delivery / oil / coolant pressure, the existing
    j1939_eflp1_* rows.
  - The initializer sets bytes 2 and 8 to 0xff and the word at bytes 5-6 to 0xffff. That word is the one 2-byte
    field, SPN 101 crankcase pressure, which was misnamed j1939_diag_response_word_b.
  - Byte 3 (SPN 98 oil level) is never written.
- j1939_pgn65504_payload_t (Proprietary B PGN 65504, 8 bytes at 0x3faeb0):
  - cm848_j1939HandlePgn65504ProprietaryB stores the engine speed and the net output torque + 0x7d7f (125 % in
    1/256 % units), each low byte first. The second word was misnamed j1939_etc1_speed_offset_tx_word.
  - Bytes 5 / 6 / 8 carry the main injection timing ((main_injection_timing + the TDC offset) x 120 >> 10 + 125),
    XORed with a key from the time base (byte 7 = TBLr >> 7) and bit-masked with fresh time base noise.
  - The two stale "+1" rows (j1939_rpm_tx_word+1 / j1939_etc1_speed_offset_tx_word+1) became dotted rows.

Proof: prove_tokens.py with the map's tokens (flattened copies) gives 1967 of 1969 functions token-equal. The two
PGN 65504 functions were read as label-substituted diffs: line wrapping, byte for undefined1, and the data pointer
now &j1939_pgn65504_payload. Pieces unchanged. Headers regenerated (0 g++ errors), lookup_tables.py 0 sizing
problems, tests pass.
