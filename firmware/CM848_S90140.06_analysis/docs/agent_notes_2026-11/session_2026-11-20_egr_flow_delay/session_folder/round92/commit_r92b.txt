cm550: round 92 stage B - the FUELING_STATE enum (CM848's twin) and its seven carriers

CM550's e2m (v64t6_s.e2m, FSFNFLST) labels fueling state codes 0x00-0x29 with the same Cummins numbering as CM848's
FUELING_STATE: 32 codes quote the same Calterm label as a CM848 member (JCOMTORQ, PTO, LIMPHOME, LSG, AFCDERAT, ...).
- FUELING_STATE (42 members, 2 bytes): those 32 codes take CM848's member names. Of the 10 codes CM848 has no member
  for, four take the label's plain words (FUELING_STATE_LOW_GEAR / _OUT_OF_GEAR / _ALTERNATE_TORQUE / _TORQUE_CONTROL)
  and six are FUELING_STATE_UNIDENTIFIED_<n> (CM848's convention), with the label in the comment (PSSPEED, ANCDERAT,
  EPF, ESUOILLM, PTPDERAT, T2DERAT).
- Carriers typed with it: user_fueling_state (FSDSFLST), requested_fueling_state (FSLMDSST) and
  fuel_demand_minimum_tracked_state (FSLMAHST). Four take CM848's names for the same e2m records:
  - final_fueling_state (FSFNFLST; was fuel_limit_selection_state);
  - adjusted_user_fueling_state (FSGVFLST) and torque_derate_fuel_source_code (FSLMFLST), which had e2m description
    names;
  - power_take_off_or_user_fueling_state (PTSLFLST; CM848 keeps it as a struct field).
  The four were untyped, so they printed with an underscore (_fuel_limit_selection_state, 40 renders).
