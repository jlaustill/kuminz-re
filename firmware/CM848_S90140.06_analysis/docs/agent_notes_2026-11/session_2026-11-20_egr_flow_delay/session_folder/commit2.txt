cm848: type the EGR transport delay lines (delay_line_t) and the signed e2m 177F words

The two helpers 0x123dc / 0x12404 store a sample at a byte index at +0x200
(lbz / stb 512(r6)) of a 256-word ring (sthx), advance the index and
return the word delay_samples calls back (lhzx / lhax at
(index - delay) * 2 & 0x1fe). Their only caller passes 0x3fa1b6 and
0x3fa3b8 (stride 0x202), so:

- delay_line_t (samples word[256] / write_index / spare) with the
  instances egr_oxygen_fraction_delay_line and egr_mass_flow_delay_line,
  and full prototypes: cm848_delayUnsignedSample(delay_line_t *, word,
  uint) -> word and cm848_delaySignedSample(delay_line_t *, sword, uint)
  -> sword. The helpers now read delay_line->samples[write_index] =
  sample; the caller passes &egr_oxygen_fraction_delay_line.
- Both lines cover a boot-ROM call target inside the kernel's RAM
  image (0x3fa2f0 cm848_runFlashProgramStateMachine, 0x3fa5a0
  mpc555_cmfEraseBlocks), so the thunk stage now gives those calls call
  overrides ("(covering) kept"; thunks 21 -> 19, overrides 11 -> 13).
  The boot calls still render by name. The type-only byte row at
  0x3fa5a0 is removed.
- The six e2m 'S' words of egr_flow_estimate_terms_t are short and the
  EGR flow limits (lha x2, e2m 177E +2 / +4 'S') are i16: 0x9c00 /
  0x8148 now read -0x6400 / -0x7eb8 and the (short) casts are gone.
- The locals of cm848_calculateDelayedEgrFlowAndFraction are named.
  Naming the reused register splits it: its later values (the two
  delays and the EGR fraction) render as an unnamed uVar1.

Changed functions vs the stage 1 build: the 6 above plus 3 whose local
declarations only reorder. Pieces 508 -> 508, build warnings unchanged,
lookup_tables.py clean, headers regenerated (g++ -fsyntax-only clean).
