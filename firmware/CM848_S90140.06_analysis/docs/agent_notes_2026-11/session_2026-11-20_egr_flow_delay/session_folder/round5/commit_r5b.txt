cm848: round 5 - all_speed_governor_gain_set_t on the three ASG gain sets; the ASG mode selectors named

all_speed_governor_gain_set_pointer points at a 9-word gain set (proportional 1 / 2, integral 1 / 2,
derivative 1 / 2, compensation filter coefficients 1-3) that cm848_runOperatingModeSpeedGovernor reads at
+0..+0x10. Three places hold one:
- the variable speed governor (throttle mode) set, e2m 107B +A..+1A (0x3ff6da);
- the ASG application set, e2m 1170 +28..+38 (0x40a232);
- a ROM table of four at 0x5c1d6 (0x5c1d6 + 4 x 18 = 0x5c21e, the next row), one per TSC1 requested speed
  control condition, indexed by etc1_speed_control_mode x 0x12: j1939_tsc1_speed_control_condition_gain_sets.
They are all_speed_governor_gain_set_t instances (short fields; the consumer loads them with lha); the 16
member rows of the two named sets go and their e2m notes move into the instance comments; the pointer is
all_speed_governor_gain_set_t *. The PID now reads all_speed_governor_gain_set_pointer->integral_gain_1 ...,
and the TSC1 selector j1939_tsc1_speed_control_condition_gain_sets[mode].integral_gain_1 (was
*(word *)(&DAT_0005c1da + iVar1)).

The two other mode selectors join cm848_selectAllSpeedGovernorApplicationMode:
cm848_initEngineOperatingMode -> cm848_selectVariableSpeedGovernorMode (state 7, the throttle-mode set) and
cm848_engineMode_reset -> cm848_selectTsc1SpeedControlMode (TSC1 speed control, the condition's set).

Proof: after reversing the labels, eight functions differ: the five intended ones (the three selectors, the
PID, the compensator coefficients reading the throttle-mode set) and three by local declaration order only.
Pieces 494 -> 494; warnings unchanged; lookup tables 0 problems; headers compile.
